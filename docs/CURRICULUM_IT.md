# Pontino — Italian curriculum map (DRAFT for approval)

Status: **Phase 0 draft. Nothing here is built yet.**

References used for scope and sequence only (no content copied): *Profilo della lingua italiana* (A1–B2 reference-level description), the *Sillabo di riferimento* of the certifying bodies (A2, B1, B2), CILS and CELI exam formats for the checkpoints.

Every unit carries: 2–4 can-do goals, one grammar focus, one interest topic, and at least one **PT tip** (Brazilian Portuguese contrast). Only the headline can-do is shown here; the full set is written with the unit.

## 1. The honest time budget

| Stage | Typical guided hours | At 15 min/day (~91 h/year) |
|---|---|---|
| A2 → B1 | ~150–200 h for most learners; usually less for Portuguese speakers | ~1.5 years, faster with weekend sessions |
| B1 → B2 | ~200–250 h | ~2 more years |

Portuguese gives you reading and vocabulary almost for free, so your comprehension will run ahead of your speaking. The risk is the opposite of Japanese: you will *understand* B1 texts early and feel fluent, while still producing Portuñol. So the course weights **output and interference drills** more than input. Conversational B2 also needs real conversation; the app can prepare you for it (role-plays, shadowing, debate prompts) but cannot replace it. I'd suggest pairing it with RaiPlay Sound listening and, later, a conversation partner.

## 2. Step 0 — Placement test (~20 min, adaptive)

| Part | Format | Decides |
|---|---|---|
| Grammar | 30 items rising A2 → B1+ (auxiliaries, pronouns, prepositions, imperfetto/passato prossimo, congiuntivo) | Which A2 review units are skipped |
| Vocabulary | 30 quick items, including false friends | Seeds SRS as *known* / *new*; seeds the false-friends deck |
| Reading | Two short texts with questions | Starting reading level |
| Listening | Four TTS clips with questions | Starting listening level |

Result: each A2 review unit is marked **skip**, **quick check** (one lesson) or **full**. Expected for a strong A2: two or three units in full.

## 3. A2 review — 6 units (only the ones placement says you need)

| Unit | Can-do (headline) | Grammar | Topic | PT tip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 Cos'hai fatto? | Tell what I did last weekend | Passato prossimo: essere vs avere, irregular participles, agreement | A weekend trip, a match day | PT uses only *ter*; **sono andato**, not *ho andato* |
| R2 Com'era? | Describe how things used to be | Imperfetto; first contrast with passato prossimo | Childhood, cities then and now | Imperfetto maps well to PT; the trap is passato prossimo vs PT *pretérito perfeito* |
| R3 Mi piace, mi è piaciuto | Talk about tastes; avoid repeating nouns | Direct/indirect pronouns; *piacere* and similar verbs | Food and restaurants | *squisito* ≠ *esquisito*; *mi piace* vs *eu gosto de* |
| R4 Al mercato, in treno | Shop and travel using natural prepositions | Preposizioni articolate; partitivi; first *ci* (place) and *ne* (quantity) | Market, Trenitalia/Italo | *nel, sul, dal* are compulsory; *ne* has no PT equivalent |
| R5 Che programmi hai? | Plan and compare | Futuro semplice; *stare* + gerundio; comparatives | Planning a route through the regions | *sto mangiando* is narrower than PT *estou comendo* |
| R6 Fallo così! + checkpoint | Give instructions and advice · A2 check | Imperativo (tu/noi/voi) with pronouns; reflexives in the past | Directions, recipes, health | Pronoun position: *dimmi, fallo, non farlo* |

## 6. Threads that run through every level

- **False-friends deck:** starts with ~60 pairs (*squisito, burro, salire, guardare, fermare, caldo, subire, prendere, largo, morbido, pronto, seta…*) and grows as units introduce new ones. Reviewed in SRS like any other card.
- **Pronunciation thread** (one micro-drill per unit): double consonants, open/closed *e* and *o*, no nasal vowels, *gli/gn*, clear final vowels, word stress.
- **"Trova l'errore" exercises:** error-correction items built from typical Brazilian-speaker mistakes.
- **Italy map:** the 20 regions colour in through B1-07, B1-11 and the weekend deep dives.
- **Politics** units cover institutions, procedures and vocabulary only. No partisan positions.
